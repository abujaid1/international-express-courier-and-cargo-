Measurement Standards

Weight & Volume Guide

International air cargo space is determined by both physical mass and cubic room occupied inside the aircraft hold.

Standard International Air Export Carton (50cm × 40cm × 30cm)

Actual Weight vs. Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight

When shipping internationally by air, the cost of transit is determined by whichever is larger: the Actual Gross Weight (what the carton weighs on a physical scale) or the Volumetric Weight (the physical volume of space the carton occupies on board the aircraft).

1. Actual Weight

The true weight of your packed parcel placed directly onto a calibrated digital warehouse scale in Kilograms (KG).

2. Volumetric Weight

Calculated from the carton’s outer Length, Width, and Height to reflect the aircraft cargo capacity utilized.

lightbulb Example Scenario:

A parcel containing light winter apparel may weigh only 4.0 kg on the physical scale, but if packed into a spacious box of 50cm × 40cm × 30cm, its space displacement yields a higher volumetric weight. The airline assesses billing on the larger figure.

Safety & Compliance Protocol

Prohibited & Restricted Items

Civil aviation security and international customs regulations strictly forbid certain materials in air courier dispatches.

battery_alert

Standalone Lithium Batteries

Loose power banks, standalone rechargeable lithium packs, and non-certified electronic power cells are barred under IATA DGR rules.

local_fire_department

Flammable & Hazardous Liquids

Perfumes with alcohol, aerosols, deodorants, lighters, paint thinners, solvent adhesives, and pressurized canisters.

payments

Currency & Bearer Securities

Hard cash, bullion, legal tender currency notes, unset precious diamonds, traveler cheques, and counterfeit securities.

set_meal

Perishables & Raw Food

Raw meats, wet dairy, fresh fruits, vegetables, and homemade unpacked food lacking commercial airtight packaging and FSSAI tags.

medication

Uncertified Medications

Narcotics, psychotropic medicines, and prescription formulations without licensed doctor's prescription, pharmacy bill, and KYC.

security

Weapons & Defense Equipment

Firearms, imitation guns, tactical knives, ammunition, defense stun guns, and explosive fireworks or pyrotechnics.

Shipper Resources

Shipping Knowledge & Guidance

Practical packing and documentation checklists to ensure friction-free international clearance.

badge

KYC & Identity Documentation

Under Indian Customs export regulations, all personal cross-border dispatches require self-attested copies of the sender's Aadhaar Card, Passport, or PAN. Commercial corporate shippers must provide GST Registration, IEC (Import Export Code), and authorized signer stamp.

mark_chat_read

Accurate Consignee Formatting

Always verify the overseas recipient's full legal name, exact street name, postal zip code, and a reachable local mobile telephone number. Delivery delays in the UAE, Europe, and USA often occur when local couriers cannot dial a working destination contact.

archive

Protective Packing Principles

Use robust double-wall corrugated cardboard boxes. Fill empty internal voids with bubble wrap or packing chips so contents cannot slide during flight turbulence. Apply high-strength vinyl packing tape in an 'H' pattern across top and bottom seams.

sync_alt

Delivery Attempts & Customs Duty

Destination customs duties (if levied by destination country customs) are the responsibility of the overseas consignee unless explicitly pre-arranged under Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) corporate freight terms. Standard couriers provide 2 to 3 delivery attempts.
