Measurement Standards

Weight & Volume Guide

International and domestic air cargo space is determined by both physical mass and cubic room occupied inside the aircraft hold.

Standard International / Domestic Air Carton (50cm × 40cm × 30cm)

Actual Weight vs. Volumetric (Dimensional) Weight

When shipping by air, the cost of transit is determined by whichever is larger: the Actual Gross Weight (what the carton weighs on a physical scale) or the Volumetric Weight (the physical volume of space the carton occupies on board the aircraft).

1. Actual Weight

The true weight of your packed parcel placed directly onto a calibrated digital warehouse scale in Kilograms (KG).

2. Volumetric Weight

Calculated from the carton’s outer Length, Width, and Height (L × W × H / 5000) to reflect aircraft space occupied.

lightbulb Example Scenario:

A parcel containing light winter apparel may weigh only 4.0 kg on the physical scale, but if packed into a spacious box of 50cm × 40cm × 30cm, its space displacement yields a higher volumetric weight (12.0 kg). Billing is assessed on the larger figure.

Assistance & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding domestic delivery timescales, air express restrictions, airport pickups, and customs clearance.

How does same-day domestic courier from Mumbai work? expand_more

Same-day delivery relies on connecting morning collections in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region directly to early afternoon commercial direct flights. Arrival at target metro terminals allows evening doorstep distribution or airport pickup. It is subject to flight availability, early booking cut-off times, and carrier security clearance.

Can you collect urgent parcels directly from Mumbai Airport (BOM)? expand_more

Yes, our Sahar Road, Andheri East hub is located adjacent to CSMI Airport. We organize expedited air-side and terminal cargo retrieval, gate clearance assistance, and priority cross-dock handoff to immediate domestic onward destinations.

What is the difference between actual scale weight and volumetric weight? expand_more

Actual weight is determined by placing the packaged parcel on a certified digital scale. Volumetric weight represents the physical air space the box occupies (calculated as Length x Width x Height in cm divided by 5000). Airlines charge based on whichever value is larger.

What paperwork is mandatory for sending goods abroad? expand_more

For personal parcels, sender self-attested Aadhaar/Passport KYC and an itemized proforma invoice are required. For commercial B2B exports, GST invoice copies, IEC certificate, export packing list, and HSN codes are required under CSB-IV / CSB-V guidelines.

How do I track both Pan-India domestic shipments and international air consignments? expand_more

Our dual consignment tracking system on this portal accepts both domestic tracking numbers (e.g. IXC-DOM-7701) and international airway bills (e.g. IXC-DEMO-001). Simply enter your docket number in the search bar above for instant real-time checkpoints.
