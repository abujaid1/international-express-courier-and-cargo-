Air Cargo Standards

Volumetric vs. Actual Weight Calculator

Airlines charge according to whichever is greater: actual gross weight or volumetric dimensional weight (L × W × H ÷ 5000 in cm).

Volumetric Weight (L×W×H÷5000): 7.20 KG
Actual Scale Weight: 5.00 KG
Billable Chargeable Weight: 7.20 KG

* Air freight standard formula applied. For oversize sea cargo or express documents, ask our operations desk.
